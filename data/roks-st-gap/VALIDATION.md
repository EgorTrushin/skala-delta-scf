## Interpretation and limits

The common-orbital and separately optimized gaps are different quantities. For Skala on its own orbitals, Molecule7 changes from a negative common splitting to a positive gap after triplet relaxation. Frozen Skala on PBE orbitals has five negative common splittings but three negative gaps (Heptazine and Molecule4 change sign). These observations are distinct from the PBE–Skala functional/orbital path analysis.

Both PBE–Skala paths remain available. Their contributions are path-dependent, not a unique decomposition. The original optimized endpoints are not replaced by the current production values; endpoint_comparison.csv records differences up to 0.000020 eV. The new full-precision parser differs from the previous iteration-row export only at the nano-eV scale.

Numerical convergence, density preservation and orbital correspondence do not establish spectroscopic assignment. Existing state-assignment qualifications remain. The near-zero Heptazine Skala@PBE gap has not been established by an independent grid/basis refinement. XC cancellation does not by itself prove the physical accuracy or identify a unique learned mechanism.

WFNs remain local and are excluded by the existing *.wfn Git pattern for inclusion in the eventual Zenodo archive. Without WFNs the parser verifies stored validation records; repeating density checks or generating probes requires them. No archive has been uploaded.
